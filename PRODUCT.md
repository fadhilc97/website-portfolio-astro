# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: hiring managers, technical leads, and recruiters evaluating Fadhil for a senior software engineering role. They arrive from an application, referral, or profile link and scan for evidence of seniority, shipped systems, and engineering standards.

Secondary: owners and managers of small and mid-sized businesses considering custom internal systems, workflow automation, operations dashboards, or integrations. They arrive from a referral or search and compare whether Fadhil can understand their operating workflow.

## Product Purpose

A bilingual personal portfolio for Fadhil, a software engineer with 6+ years of experience. It exists to make real shipped systems and engineering judgment legible enough to start a conversation. Success is a visitor opening a WhatsApp conversation; for the employment audience that means a hiring conversation, and for the business audience it means a project discussion.

## Positioning

Fadhil builds custom digital systems shaped around a client's actual business workflow rather than adopting an off-the-shelf product. The claim is backed by two publicly linked, working systems: an industrial-readiness assessment platform and a residential rental management system. The differentiator is workflow analysis plus production-grade implementation, not a fixed service catalog or template.

## Operating Context

- The site is bilingual: English at `/`, Indonesian at `/id/`, with parallel routes, anchors, and copy.
- Contact runs through WhatsApp (`wa.me/6281372079915`) and email (`fadhilc97@gmail.com`); there is no form or account system.
- Case-study CTAs open externally hosted live demos at `readiness.incit.org` and `kost-app.fadhildev.com`.
- The site is a static Astro build deployed on push to `main` through GitHub Actions over SSH to a self-managed VPS.
- The repository is the rewrite of an earlier vanilla HTML/CSS/JS site; the Astro component structure and the incumbent visual styling are intentional, not provisional.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Four service areas: custom internal systems, workflow automation, digitalized operations management, and system integrations.
- A four-stage engagement workflow: discovery and analysis, architecture and design, development and testing, handover and support.
- Two case studies, each with a public live demo and a screenshot.
- Technical: Astro 7 static output, Node `>=22.23.4`, no backend or CMS, deploy on push to `main`.
- Open decision: the primary audience is employment-first, but the current copy and service framing address business owners. Whether to rebalance the copy, add a role-seeking path, or keep the dual framing is not decided.
- Open decision: no testimonials or quantified outcome metrics are available; future work must not add them.

## Brand Commitments

- The brand name is "Fadhil"; there is no separate product or agency name, and no logo asset beyond the wordmark.
- Voice is professional, direct, and outcome-focused, in both languages.
- English and Indonesian versions are kept in parity; one locale must not be treated as an afterthought.
- The two case studies, their screenshots, and their live demo links are approved public assets.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real: 6+ years of software engineering experience (confirmed).
- Real: two case studies with public live demos — Project Readiness Index (`https://readiness.incit.org`) and the residential rental management system (`https://kost-app.fadhildev.com`).
- Assets: `public/assets/images/professional-profile.png`, `public/assets/images/project-readiness-index.png`, `public/assets/images/project-residential-app.png`.
- Absent and not to be fabricated: client testimonials, quantified results or metrics, additional client names beyond what the case studies already publish, certifications, and press mentions.

## Product Principles

1. **Show working systems, not assertions.** Every capability claim should be traceable to a live demo, a shipped case study, or a stated fact.
2. **Lead with seniority and judgment.** The employment-first audience must see experience, technical standards, and real delivery before anything else.
3. **Make the next step one move.** Contact stays a single, obvious action; no unnecessary funnel between interest and a conversation.
4. **Treat both languages as the product.** English and Indonesian stay structurally and editorially aligned.
5. **Never inflate proof.** Absent testimonials, metrics, and client names stay absent until they are real.
